# Rankin Realms — Privacy Policy Draft

Rankin Realms is designed to work without an account. The MVP does not collect contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or the user's photo library.

The app connects to the internet to retrieve the artwork catalog, preview images, and full-resolution masters selected by the user. Downloaded masters may be cached privately on the device for faster reuse and can be cleared from Settings.

When the user chooses to save a master outside the app, Android's system document picker is used so the user selects the destination directly. The app does not request broad photo or media-library access for this action.

When the user chooses to apply a wallpaper, the app sends the rendered wallpaper image to Android's WallpaperManager for Home screen, Lock screen, or both.
